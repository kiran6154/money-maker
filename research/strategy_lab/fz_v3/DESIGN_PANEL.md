## Judges' verdicts

### Judge 1

Skeptical-practitioner verdict. The tape sets the ceiling before any model runs: 1m Foundation is +1.9 pts per trade gross against ~16 pts of cost (mean net -922 INR, 15.2% winners, 4,502 IS SETUPs in 579 active sessions); 5m is 184 IS winners with the top decile carrying ~46% of gross and an IS-to-OOS mean shift from -277 to -1,060 per trade. So any gate must find a subset whose expectancy clears ~16-20 pts, and the whole signal lives in a few hundred trades on 1m and a few dozen on 5m. On that sample the block-bootstrap CI of a kept-vs-skipped difference will be several hundred INR per trade wide; most candidates will not clear it, and that is the honest expected outcome. Every library the 18 studies name is present (sklearn 1.7.2 with interaction_cst/monotonic_cst, numba 0.65, xgboost 3.2.0, shap 0.49.1, pyarrow, scipy, matplotlib) and statsmodels/torch/mapie are absent as assumed; feasibility failures are compute and memory, not imports.

Keep seven, merged into five lines of work, about 1-1.5 CPU-hours per timeframe in total: (1) one validation harness = quant-ml-canon-purged-cpcv-pbo with decision-making-3's SPA, adversarial drift and 5m null tapes folded in (one ledger, one bootstrap, one PBO, active sessions only, every comparator re-priced on the L1 15:25 label); (2) one feature-vocabulary pass = quant-ml-canon-feature-importance, its 8-cluster shortlist frozen in the ledger before any gate search, with BOCPD, FFD and the novelty scalars entering there and nowhere else; (3) one learned-gate family = quant-ml-canon-meta-label-gate and decision-making-1 merged (L1 label, capped |net| weights, top-1%-winners-removed as a hard pass, block-wise sign test, one distilled rule list or scorecard as the single OOS candidate from the family); (4) the interpretable candidate = deep-sequence-llm-hypotheses with a blind round 0 from the user's words alone, hashed before any table is shown, and cross-fitted round-1 tables; (5) the cheap desk rule = decision-making-6 session stop with an hour-conditioned null. That yields at most three OOS looks: the rule list from (3), the round-0 list from (4), and the frozen ST7/ST8 gate as comparator, with (5) as a multiplication if its null is rejected.

Drop or defer eleven. The sequence and representation studies (rocket, pretrain, HMM/jump, motif/shapelet, vision) share one structural flaw: the only shippable form is a depth-3 tree on the same scalar features the tabular studies already search, so they add trials to the ledger without adding a candidate, and the pre-SETUP window is templated by the engine's own SETUP definition; pretrain and 1m FQI also need 750-900 MB for hours on a box shared with another build. The two conformal studies and the two sizing studies do not change which SETUPs are taken: they choose a threshold on a score another study produces, or size a positive edge that does not yet exist; their useful pieces (slippage 3/5/8 sensitivity, Pareto front with user-chosen w, |net|-weighted coverage, one-lot-or-skip verdict) become sections of the gate study's deliverable. The regime-breaks study keeps only its break tests as the harness's pre-registration step (they decide time-decay weights and the OOS caveat); BSADF is a feature candidate for the importance pass, not a gate. The exit study is worth its parametric learner C (config keys the lab already has, 10 minutes by replay) but must be coordinated with the ST9-12 session, which already ran an exit grid on 2026.

Two standing caveats every OOS table must carry: the OOS window (2026-01-01..09-25) is also the published lab dashboard window and was used by the ST9-12 exit grid, so it is not pristine; and the 5m Foundation label as built holds 358 of 1,012 trades past the entry session, so ST13/ST14 (square_off 15:25) must be judged on the recomputed intraday label and every comparator re-priced on it.

### Judge 2

Twelve of eighteen studies are worth running here; six are cut or folded. The binding facts: (1) sample size, not compute, is the limit: 684 IS winners on 1m and 184 on 5m, a 15-22% win rate, mean net -922 INR per trade against ~1,050 INR costs, so a gate must find a subset averaging > 16 pts, and the top-decile winners are half of gross wins; every learned number must therefore be reported as a CPCV path distribution (5th percentile), with |net| weights winsorised and kept-n floors. (2) memory: lab.py holds 1.24 GB and 759 MB is free, so the '2 processes under 1 GB' budget is currently one process under ~600 MB; the rocket probe at 10k kernels, the pretrain ladder, 1m FQI and 1m synthetic tapes do not fit until lab.py exits. (3) libraries: everything named that is present imports (numpy, pandas, scipy, sklearn 1.7.2 with interaction_cst/monotonic_cst, numba 0.65, xgboost 3.2.0, shap 0.49.1, pyarrow, matplotlib, PIL, joblib); statsmodels, torch, mapie, hmmlearn, ruptures, arch are absent and every study that names them correctly hand-codes. Structural fixes that apply across the set: there must be ONE harness (merge quant-ml-canon-purged-cpcv-pbo with decision-making-3's bootstrap/PBO): 12 contiguous IS session blocks, purge by the label's own exit bar, 3-session embargo, one append-only ledger reached only through a score() function, PBO <= 0.2, kept-share/kept-n floors, slippage-8-pt survival, and ONE OOS script run once for at most 3 frozen candidates; all eighteen 'OOS once' clauses collapse into that, otherwise OOS is opened eighteen times. Four near-duplicate pairs merge: meta-label + policy-tree gate (the central study, EV 4), conformal-skip + conformal-risk-control (one operating-point step the user chooses on), the two sizing studies (conditional on a gate surviving; prior is 'one lot or skip'), the two harnesses. The L1 intraday (15:25) label written once is the only training label; the engine label's multi-session holds (358 of 1,012 on 5m) make purging by it too costly. Regime, novelty and state-model outputs enter as candidate features into the importance study rather than as separate gate searches, which is the cheapest cut in multiplicity. The LLM-hypothesis study needs a blind round 0 and a trials count equal to the labelled cells shown; the vision study runs as a 40-session pilot against mechanical proxies first; both must be separate agents from any study author. Cut: pretrain-probe (3-4 h, 750 MB, negative result foreseen), motif/shapelet (keep only the causal novelty score), rocket as proposed (reduce to a 5-minute ceiling check), both sizing studies as standalone. Run order: break tests and L1 label, harness, importance, merged gate, operating point, exit learners C and B (FQI on 5m only), session-stop, BOCPD/GMM regime, LLM rounds, vision pilot, null tapes on 5m; everything above 600 MB waits for lab.py.

## Designers' rationales

- What this lens can honestly do on this box. The brief's data now exists in the session scratchpad (fz_v3/5minute complete; fz_v3/minute engine/trades written, features running): 5m has 1,012 Foundation SETUPs (832 IS / 180 OOS, 22% net winners, mean net -277 INR), 1m has 5,326 (4,502 IS / 824 OOS, 15% winners, mean net -922 INR, sd ~5,300 INR, median hold 15 bars, p90 92). So the supervised sample is 4.5k rows with ~680 positive labels on 1m and 830 rows with 184 positives on 5m; OOS confirmation rests on 128 and 37 winners respectively. Standard error of an OOS mean net is ~185 INR/trade (1m) and ~375 INR/trade (5m): a gate must move kept-vs-skipped expectancy by roughly 500 (1m) / 1,000 (5m) INR per trade to be visible at all, and nothing subtler is decidable on this tape. Every study below is sized to that reality: the models are feature extractors and regime detectors whose output is distilled into a compact rule list, not end-to-end deep classifiers.

Library reality, checked with `python -c import`: torch, tensorflow, hmmlearn, stumpy, ruptures, tslearn, sktime, pyts are absent and nothing may be installed. Present: numpy 2.2, sklearn 1.7.2, pandas 2.3, scipy 1.14, numba 0.65, xgboost 3.2, shap 0.49, matplotlib 3.9, PIL, pyarrow, psutil. Consequences, stated per study: a gradient-trained 1D-CNN/GRU/Transformer is not possible without a framework (a hand-written numpy CNN is a day of build for no expected gain at n=4.5k); its honest modern substitute is the random-convolutional-kernel transform (ROCKET/MiniRocket) plus a linear probe, which the literature shows matches small CNNs on small time-series benchmarks and which I benchmarked here at 2.1 s per 1,500 windows x 2,000 kernels. Contrastive pretraining (InfoNCE) needs a custom loss, so the self-supervised study uses the ladder PCA -> sklearn MLP autoencoder -> forecast-pretext encoder and reports whether anything beats PCA. Matrix profile, HMM, statistical jump model and Bayesian online change-point detection are all implementable in numpy/numba in under 150 lines each and were timed (FFT distance profile 0.19 s per query over the whole 445k-bar 1m tape; GMM on 90k x 6 in 51 s).

Machine: 4 cores, 7.9 GB total but only ~0.9 GB free at the time of writing (lab.py rebuild holds 1.5 GB, the 1m feature build ~200 MB), so studies must run one at a time until the rebuild finishes, and every study is designed to peak under 800 MB with 1m windows stored as float32 and never more than ~150k pretraining windows in memory at once.

Common protocol shared by all six (each study's leakage_controls field adds the study-specific parts): (1) unit = Foundation SETUP with its own engine trade from the build (fnd_net_inr, fnd_win, exit reason); (2) every feature/window uses bars <= setup_i (the SETUP bar's close is the entry; the window ends at k inclusive); (3) IS-only learning with purged, embargoed, session-blocked CV: 5 contiguous chronological blocks of ~205 IS sessions, training SETUPs whose Foundation exit session falls in the test block or its embargo are purged, embargo = max sessions_held observed on that timeframe (+1), all normalisation/PCA/kernel-bias/model parameters fit inside the training folds; (4) OOS (2026-01-01..2026-09-25) is scored once by a single logged script run with the model frozen on all IS, never re-tuned; (5) judgement is kept-vs-skipped mean-net difference with fz_report.permutation_p, the session-matched random control percentile (fz_report.random_control; the current ST7/ST8 sit at the 39.6th/57th percentile, so the bar is 'above the 95th'), precision/recall on losers, and the share of top-decile winners skipped; net alone is never the criterion because every gate that trades less banks ~1,114 INR per skipped 1m trade; (6) multiplicity: every configuration tried is counted and a max-T permutation across the family (White's Reality Check style) is reported next to the single-rule p; (7) output = a candidate ST13/ST14 gate block: <= 8 conjunctive rules of depth <= 3 over named as-of features with provenance 'learned on IS 2021-10..2025-12, script <name>, sha'; any non-scalar artefact (HMM parameters, a shapelet template) is offered only as an alternative that needs a user decision on a new fz key and code contract.

Ordering: the LLM-hypothesis study is first because it is config-native, cheap and directly encodes the user's own reads; the regime-state study second because 'sideways hurts' is the user's central claim and the models are small enough to ship; the random-kernel probe third as the cheapest ceiling test for 'is there information in the bar shape at all'; vision labels fourth because they give the user's visual notion of sideways a ground truth (and the first number, Foundation expectancy by hindsight label, decides whether any causal detector is worth building); motif/shapelet fifth; self-supervised pretraining last because without a framework it is the most likely to report 'nothing beyond PCA'. Sizing and exits: studies 2 and 3 emit a per-SETUP expectancy score; the brief's exits stay Foundation's, so sizing appears only as a secondary output (lots 0/1/2 by score tercile) judged with the same control; a during-trade version of the window models for early exits is a natural follow-on once the position-path table exists, and is not proposed here.

- Lens and framing. Foundation is the primary model: it fixes side, entry bar and exit rule for every SETUP, and (decisively) its outcome is observed for every SETUP in the data, taken or not. So the take/skip/size decision at the SETUP bar is a one-step problem with full-information feedback: it is supervised meta-labelling in Lopez de Prado's sense, not a bandit (no off-policy correction is needed because the counterfactual is in the table) and not sequential RL (no position overlap: 0 of 1,003 Foundation trades overlap on 5m, by construction the next SETUP needs the CHoCH that exits the previous trade). Everything proposed below descends from that framing: the labels and weights (AFML ch. 3-4), the validation harness (ch. 7, 11-14), importance with substitution effects removed (ch. 8; MLAM ch. 6), calibrated sizing (ch. 10), conformal sets for the skip decision (Vovk; Angelopoulos and Bates), and break/explosiveness statistics as regime features (ch. 17). Sample-size reality drives the design: on 5m IS there are 823 SETUPs and 181 winners; the engine-label net has std 10,271 INR (holds run to 70 sessions because the engine has no square-off), so a kept-vs-skipped expectancy difference below about 1,400 INR per trade (400 vs 400 split, 5% two-sided) is undetectable on that label. The intraday label (exit forced at the entry session's 15:25 bar, the lab's ST1/ST2 book) is therefore the primary label in every study; the top decile of winners carries 46% of gross wins (win mean +10,878, loss mean -3,429), so every gate is judged on |net|-weighted winner recall and kept-vs-skipped expectancy plus the session-matched random control, never on accuracy or net alone (a gate that trades less always saves costs). Data facts the studies rely on: the 5-minute table at scratchpad/fz_v3/5minute (features.parquet, 186 columns, 143 as-of; 97 numeric with 114 pairs |rho| > 0.9; causality verified by the truncated build), the 1-minute table not yet landed (scripts 01-03 only) but promised with the same column contract (a few thousand SETUPs). Box: 4 logical cores, 7.9 GB RAM with about 1.2 GB free beside the other session's 1.1 GB lab process; every study is a single process under 500 MB and 25 CPU-minutes per timeframe, at most two at a time, no pip installs (numpy 2.2.6, sklearn 1.7.2, pandas 2.3.3, scipy 1.14.1, xgboost 3.2.0, shap 0.49.1, numba 0.65, joblib; statsmodels/mapie/mlfinlab/lightgbm/torch absent, so ADF, CUSUM, FFD, conformal and PBO are hand-coded in numpy, all short). Order and dependencies: the harness (study 2) first because every other study and H1-H5 consume its splitter and trials ledger; then meta-labelling (1) which also hands H5 its labels, weights, OOF probabilities and splitter instead of a second gradient-boosting run; importance (3) and regime (6) can run in parallel with (1) since they only need the splitter; conformal (4) and sizing (5) consume (1)'s OOF file and are cheap. Deliberately left out with reasons: sequential bootstrap (uniqueness is 1 by construction for Foundation labels; only the triple-barrier variants with vertical barriers overlap and those get the concurrency weights inside study 1); HRP / portfolio construction (one instrument); deep sequence models and transformers (1k-5k labels, no GPU, and nothing to distil into 8 rules); sequential RL (one-step decision with the counterfactual observed); generative AI in the LLM sense (no standing in the canon at this sample size; the only generative element the canon endorses here is the CPCV path distribution and the stationary block bootstrap of sessions in study 2, which is what gives the confidence intervals). OOS discipline shared by all six: candidate configs and the exact scoring scripts are frozen to JSON with a sha before any OOS row is read; the harness pre-registers the go/no-go rule (PBO <= 0.2, CPCV 5th-percentile kept-vs-skipped difference > 0, deflated-Sharpe p < 0.1, at most three candidates across all lenses get the one OOS look); the frozen ST7/ST8 gate is always one of the comparators; ST13/ST14 must carry square_off "15:25" so that the traded book matches the label the gate was learned on.

- Why this lens splits the problem the way it does. (1) The take/skip choice at a Foundation SETUP is a contextual bandit with FULL information: Foundation took every SETUP, so the reward of "take" (fnd_net, 1m: 5,326 rows, 5m: 1,012 rows) is logged for every context, and the reward of "skip" is exactly 0 by definition. There is no unobserved arm, hence no propensity model, no inverse-propensity weighting and no doubly-robust correction: the off-policy value of ANY gate pi is computed exactly as sum over SETUPs of pi(x)*net(x). The only statistical problem is generalisation from a few thousand contexts, so the right tools are cost-sensitive policy learning (value-maximising policy trees, cost-proportionate weighting) and honest selection, not bandit exploration. (2) Sequential RL is genuinely justified only where today's action changes which future rewards remain reachable: the in-trade exit (hold/exit/partial each bar; 1m IS has ~186k intraday bar-steps inside positions, ~800k once trajectories are extended to the 15:25 square-off) and, at session level, whether to keep trading today. On a price-taker's tape the dynamics are exogenous (an Exo-MDP): any exit policy can be replayed exactly and the hindsight-optimal exit is computable per trade, so OPE (DR/MAGIC) is not the bottleneck; generalisation and multiplicity are. Deep offline RL (CQL, IQL, Decision Transformer) and neural generators (TimeGAN, diffusion) are not runnable here (no torch) and would be badly over-parameterised for 4.5k trajectories anyway; the studies say so and use the closest feasible analogues (pessimistic tree-ensemble FQI, hindsight-imitation, GMM-Markov and session-bootstrap tapes). (3) Sizing is a downstream Kelly problem that only exists once a positive-conditional-edge region is found; the raw book (win rate 15%, mean -922 INR) has negative Kelly, and the study is written to return "0 lots" honestly. (4) Costs are 1,050-1,120 INR per trade and the median 1m trade moves 9 pts, so a gate that trades less always raises net by the saved costs: every study judges on kept-vs-skipped expectancy, the session-matched random control at the same per-session position count (fz_report.random_control), the permutation p, and (new here) the multiplicity-adjusted SPA/PBO from session-block resampling. (5) Leakage discipline is shared: features from the as-of columns only (prefixes fnd_, fwd_, fzpos_, st7_ POST columns, swings.broken_final, events.window_end/end, zones/visits final-state fields are excluded), contiguous session-block CV with purge (drop training trades whose exit falls in the test block) and a 2-session embargo, OOS (2026-01-01..2026-09-25) touched once per study at the end; the 5-minute engine trades are re-cut at 15:25 from bars because the build carries no square-off (358 of 1,012 held overnight). Priority order: the gate (1), the exit policy (2), then the robustness/multiplicity certificate that must pass before any key ships (3), the certified operating point (4), sizing (5), the session-stop rule (6). Compute: every study fits in one process under 1 GB; the two heavy jobs (1m FQI ensemble, 1m synthetic tapes) must not run concurrently with each other because each peaks near 900 MB.

## Studies

### deep-sequence-llm-hypotheses (priority 1)

**technique.** LLM-as-hypothesis-generator with pre-registration and a family-wise permutation test: Claude reads IS-only summaries of the per-SETUP feature table and writes a fixed, hashed list of candidate skip rules before any rule is scored.

**what_it_answers.** Does a small set of human-readable rules, written from the user's own observations (sideways after repeated CHoCH, reaction to visibly high volume, respected vs broken levels, time of day, room read) plus what the IS marginals show, skip losers without skipping winners; and is the effect real after correcting for having proposed several rules at once? This is the only study whose raw output is already in ST13/ST14 config form.

**method.** Inputs: fz_v3/<tf>/features.csv (IS rows only), the README feature dictionary, and the H1-H4 findings when available. Step 1, summaries the generator may see (script deep_llm_summaries.py): per as-of feature, decile table of Foundation mean net, win rate and count on IS; two-way tables for read x hour, n_choch_since_bos x dir, hv3_dir_agree x hv3_bars_since bucket, fz_visit_n x fz_read; nothing from OOS, no raw rows. Step 2, proposal: Claude writes deep_llm_rules_round1.json with <= 8 rules, each a conjunction of <= 3 comparisons on named features (e.g. {n_choch_since_bos >= 2, bars_since_bos > 36, range36_atr < 1.5} -> skip), with the reason in the user's words; the file's sha256 and timestamp are logged (deep_llm_register.txt) before step 3 runs. Step 3, scoring (deep_llm_eval.py): each rule on 5-fold purged session-blocked IS CV: kept n, skipped n, mean net kept vs skipped, permutation p (fz_report.permutation_p with 2,000 draws), losers-recall / precision, share of top-decile winners skipped, and the session-matched control percentile of the kept set; then a max-T permutation across all rules in the round (the family-wise p for the best rule), and Holm-adjusted single p's. Step 4: rules with Holm p < 0.05 and control percentile >= 95 on CV are combined greedily (order by CV kept-vs-skipped gain, stop when the marginal rule's gain < 200 INR/trade or when winners skipped rises) into a rule list of <= 8; a second round is allowed only if it is registered as round 2 and the multiplicity count carries over. Step 5: the frozen list scored once on OOS with the same table. Outputs: the round files, the CV table per rule, the OOS table, and the candidate ST13/ST14 gate block; a null result is reported as such.

**leakage_controls.** Generator sees IS aggregates only, never OOS and never row-level forward returns; the proposal file is hashed before scoring so no rule is edited after seeing its number; features are the build's as-of columns only (fzpost_*, fwd_*, fnd_* excluded by column-name allow-list enforced in code); CV is purged/embargoed session-blocked; multiplicity is counted over every rule ever proposed across rounds and reported as a max-T permutation p; OOS run once, logged.

**compute.** Under 5 CPU-minutes per timeframe (pandas tables and 2,000-draw permutations for <= 16 rules); peak memory under 300 MB. The real cost is Claude tokens for reading the summaries (~30k per timeframe).

**libraries.** pandas, numpy; fz_report.permutation_p and fz_report.random_control imported read-only from the repo for the exact control definitions.

**output_as_config.** Directly the ST13/ST14 gate block: {"gate": {"minute": {"rules": [{"if": [["n_choch_since_bos", ">=", 2], ["bars_since_bos", ">", 36]], "then": "skip", "source": "learned on IS 2021-10..2025-12, script deep_llm_eval.py, sha <..>, round 1 rule 3", "statistic": "CV kept-vs-skipped +A INR/trade, Holm p, control pct"}]}}}; each feature named must be one the fz/lab code can compute as-of at the SETUP bar, and the list is <= 8 rules of depth <= 3 as the brief requires.

**novelty.** LLMs as zero-shot hypothesis proposers over tabular science data (Qi et al. 2023; Zhou et al. 2024 'Hypothesis generation with large language models'; FunSearch, Romera-Paredes et al. Nature 2024, for LLM-proposed heuristics scored by a fixed evaluator), disciplined by pre-registration (Nosek et al. 2018) against the garden of forking paths (Gelman & Loken 2013) and by the finance-native family-wise tests for data-snooped trading rules (White 2000 Reality Check; Hansen 2005 SPA; Westfall & Young max-T).

**Judge 1** feasible 5 · rigour 3 · expected value 3 · keep True — fix: Trap: the generator writes rules after reading decile and two-way tables of IS mean net by feature, so the real family is every table cell it saw, not the <= 8 rules it wrote, and the 5-fold CV table is decoration for thresholds chosen on all of IS (garden of forking paths moved into the prompt). Fix: round 0 = rules from the user's words and the README feature dictionary only, hashed before any table is shown (this is H2-H4 in config form and the deliverable even if round 1 looks better); round 1 = tables built on IS blocks 1-6 only, scored on blocks 7-12 and vice versa (cross-fitted), with the number of table cells shown logged as the family size for max-T; both rounds through the harness ledger; features restricted to study 3's shortlist.

**Judge 2** feasible 5 · rigour 3 · expected value 3 · keep True — fix: The generator sees decile and two-way tables of the IS label, so the proposal is data-derived and the max-T over 8 rules undercounts the search. Fix: round 0 is blind (rules written from the user's words and the feature dictionary only, hashed before any labelled table exists); round 1 may see labelled tables, and its trials count in the ledger/PBO is the number of table cells shown (~1,000+), not 8. Use the harness splitter (12 blocks, 3-session embargo) instead of a private 5-fold. The generator agent must be a different agent from the vision labeller and from any study author that saw OOF scores. Compute < 5 min.

### deep-sequence-regime-states (priority 2)

**technique.** Latent regime states from per-bar features: a hand-rolled Gaussian HMM (Baum-Welch, causal forward filtering at inference), a statistical jump model (k-means with a jump penalty, online prefix version), a GaussianMixture no-dynamics baseline, and Bayesian online change-point detection (BOCPD) run-length features; all fit on IS bars only.

**what_it_answers.** Is there a machine-found 'chop' state that marks losing Foundation SETUPs better than the hand rule H2 tests (n_choch_since_bos >= k), how many bars of confirmation it needs (the filtered state lags), and whether 'time since the last structural change' (BOCPD run length) carries the same information more cheaply. Also gives the vision study (below) a mechanical comparator.

**method.** Per-bar as-of feature vector x_t from fz_v3/<tf>/bars.csv and events.csv: |log return|, (high-low)/atr14, |close-open|/(high-low), log(volume/median volume of the previous 20 bars), events (BOS+CHoCH) in the last 36 bars, CHoCHs since the last BOS at t, range of the last 36 bars / atr14, signed 36-bar return / atr14; features are z-scored with IS statistics. Models: (a) Gaussian HMM with K in {3,4}, diagonal covariance, Baum-Welch 50 iterations in numba, initialised from k-means, fit on IS bars of the training folds; at inference only the forward (filtered) posterior P(s_t | x_<=t) is used; the smoothed posterior is computed too but only as a hindsight diagnostic. (b) Statistical jump model: minimise sum ||x_t - mu_{s_t}||^2 + lambda * 1[s_t != s_{t-1}] by alternating centroid updates and Viterbi DP, lambda from a small grid chosen on CV; the online state is the prefix-DP argmax. (c) sklearn GaussianMixture(K) on the same vectors as the no-dynamics baseline. (d) BOCPD (Adams & MacKay) with a normal-inverse-gamma predictive on 1m/5m log returns and separately on log range, hazard 1/H for H in {60, 240} bars, run-length truncated at 400; features: MAP run length, P(run length < 10), bars since the last MAP reset. At each SETUP bar k the study attaches: P(state = c | x_<=k) for each state, the jump-model state, and the BOCPD features. Analysis: Foundation net and win rate by filtered state and by BOCPD run-length bucket on IS; identify the state with the worst expectancy (the candidate chop state) inside each training fold; gate = skip when P(chop) >= tau (tau grid 0.5..0.9) or run length >= r; evaluate on purged session-blocked CV against H2's rule at matched skip fraction (the HMM must beat the hand rule's kept-vs-skipped difference at the same trade count, else it is not worth its parameters); final model refit on all IS; OOS once. Outputs: state description tables (means per feature), transition matrix, the expectancy-by-state table on IS CV and OOS, the control percentile, and the distilled rule.

**leakage_controls.** Only filtered (forward) posteriors and prefix-DP states are used at SETUP bars; smoothed posteriors and full-sequence Viterbi paths are labelled hindsight and never enter a feature; all model parameters and z-scoring statistics are estimated on IS training-fold bars only, refit per fold; BOCPD is causal by construction; the chop state is identified per training fold, not on the test fold; embargo covers the longest Foundation hold; OOS bars are only ever filtered through the frozen IS model.

**compute.** 5m: HMM 76k bars x 8 features x 4 states x 50 EM iterations in numba ~1 min per fold, 5 folds ~5 min; 1m 445k bars ~5 min per fold, ~25 min; jump model similar; GMM ~1 min per fit (benchmarked 51 s for 90k x 6 x 4 components); BOCPD 445k x 400 run lengths in numba ~1 min. Total ~40-60 CPU-minutes per timeframe; peak memory under 400 MB (bars as float32, per-bar posteriors K x N float32).

**libraries.** numpy, numba (HMM forward/backward, jump-model DP, BOCPD), sklearn.mixture.GaussianMixture and sklearn.cluster.KMeans for initialisation, pandas.

**output_as_config.** Preferred: a distilled rule list over scalar as-of features that reproduces the chop-state gate (a depth-3 tree fit to predict 'P(chop) >= tau' from the same per-bar features must reach >= 85% fidelity on CV, else the study says the state is not expressible compactly). Alternative needing a user decision: an fz block key such as {"regime": {"model": "hmm_filtered", "features": [...], "means": [[...]], "vars": [[...]], "transition": [[...]], "chop_state": 2, "skip_if_p_chop_ge": 0.7}} (about 40-60 numbers for K=4 x 8 features) plus a forward-filter routine in fz code; the BOCPD variant is two numbers (hazard, run-length threshold) and is compact by itself.

**novelty.** Markov-switching regimes (Hamilton 1989) and Gaussian HMMs (Rabiner 1989) are classical; the current pieces are statistical jump models for regime detection with an online variant (Bemporad et al. 2018; Nystrup, Kolm & Lindstrom 2020/2021, 'sparse jump models'), Bayesian online change-point detection (Adams & MacKay 2007) as a causal alternative to offline change-point search (ruptures, Truong et al. 2020), and the discipline of separating filtered from smoothed states (the look-ahead trap in regime backtests documented by Nystrup et al.).

Fix: run only BOCPD (two numbers, causal, one minute) and hand run-length features to study 3; run the HMM only if study 3 shows the H2 counts are not already the regime cluster, and then on 5m only.

**Judge 2** feasible 4 · rigour 4 · expected value 3 · keep True — fix: hmmlearn is absent, so Baum-Welch with scaling is hand-coded in numba (~150 lines, real bug risk): run the cheap rungs first, BOCPD (2-number config, numba, ~1 min) and sklearn GaussianMixture (benchmarked 51 s), and fit the HMM/jump model only if the GMM state already shows a kept-vs-skipped signal at matched skip fraction versus n_choch_since_bos on CV. ### deep-sequence-rocket-probe (priority 3)

**technique.** Random convolutional kernel transform (MiniRocket-style: thousands of fixed dilated 1D kernels, proportion-of-positive-values pooling) over the multichannel pre-SETUP bar window, then a ridge/logistic linear probe; compared with an sklearn MLP on the same window and with the H5 tabular gradient-boosting reference; then distilled to rules.

**what_it_answers.** Whether the shape of the last 60-120 bars (1m) / 12-36 bars (5m) before a SETUP carries any information about the Foundation outcome beyond the hand-built as-of features; how large the ceiling is (AUC and kept-vs-skipped expectancy at fixed skip fractions); and whether the part that matters can be written as a few threshold rules on scalar features.

**method.** Window tensor per SETUP: L bars ending at setup_i inclusive (1m: L in {60, 120}; 5m: L in {12, 36}), channels: sg x log return per bar (direction-normalised so up and down SETUPs pool), (high-low)/atr14[k], sg x (close-open)/atr14[k], log(volume/vol_med20[k]) with NA -> 0 plus an NA flag channel, sg x (close - choch_lvl)/atr14[k], and a same-session indicator; stored float32. Transform: 5,000-10,000 random kernels of length 9, weights in {-1, 2} centred, dilations sampled log-uniformly up to L/4, one channel or a random pair per kernel, bias drawn from quantiles of the training-fold convolution outputs (as MiniRocket does), PPV pooling (numba, benchmarked 2.1 s per 1,500 windows x 2,000 kernels x 60 bars). Probes: RidgeClassifierCV on the PPV features (alpha grid 1e-3..1e3), L2 logistic regression for calibrated probabilities, and ridge regression on winsorised net (+-10,000 INR). Comparators inside the same CV folds: sklearn MLPClassifier(64,16) on the flattened z-scored window, H5's xgboost on tabular as-of features, and the stacked model (tabular + top PCA components of the PPV features). Selection on 5-fold purged session-blocked IS CV by: AUC, mean net of the kept set at skip fractions 30/50/70%, kept-vs-skipped difference, and the pooled session-matched control percentile of the kept set; incremental value = stacked minus tabular. Distillation: the probe score's bottom tercile ('predicted loser') becomes a binary target and a depth-3 decision tree on scalar as-of features (tabular + a handful of window summaries such as the sign-agreement count of the last 10 bars, the drawdown from the window high, the bar-range trend) is fit to it; the tree's rules are the config candidate if fidelity >= 85% and the rule's own CV kept-vs-skipped difference keeps >= 70% of the probe's. Final: refit on all IS, one OOS run. Outputs: the CV/OOS table for each model, calibration plot data, the distilled rules, and the statement of how much of the effect the rules keep.

**leakage_controls.** Windows end at the SETUP bar; kernel biases, channel scaling and PCA are fit on training folds only, inside the CV loop; labels (fnd_net_inr) never enter the transform; purged/embargoed session-blocked CV; the number of window lengths x kernel counts x probes tried is recorded and a max-T permutation over that family is reported; OOS once with the frozen kernel set (seeds logged) and frozen ridge weights. Direction-normalisation is stated per channel so that no channel silently encodes a forward quantity.

**compute.** Transform: 5,326 windows x 6 channels x 120 bars x 10,000 kernels ~90 s (+21 s JIT) per fold-fit of biases; RidgeCV on 5,326 x 10,000 ~2-5 min per fit; 5 folds x 2 window lengths x 3 probes ~45-75 CPU-minutes for 1m, ~10 min for 5m; MLP comparator ~5 min. Peak memory ~600 MB (feature matrix 5,326 x 10,000 float32 = 213 MB, doubled by sklearn's float64 copy; use 5,000 kernels if the lab rebuild is still holding memory).

**libraries.** numpy, numba (kernel transform), sklearn (RidgeClassifierCV, LogisticRegression, MLPClassifier, DecisionTreeClassifier, PCA), xgboost for the H5 comparator, shap optional for the stacked model's attribution.

**output_as_config.** Only the distilled rule list is proposed for ST13/ST14 (<= 8 rules of depth <= 3 over as-of scalar features, provenance 'distilled from rocket probe, script deep_rocket.py, sha'); the kernel bank and ridge weights (~10k numbers) are not compact and are explicitly not offered as config. If the probe shows a ceiling the rules cannot reach, the study reports the gap in INR/trade and stops.

**novelty.** ROCKET (Dempster, Petitjean & Webb, DMKD 2020), MiniRocket (KDD 2021), MultiRocket (2022) and Hydra (2023) are the current state of the art for small-data time-series classification on CPU and descend from random-feature methods (Rahimi & Recht 2007) and untrained convolutional features; the linear-probe evaluation protocol is SimCLR's (Chen et al. 2020). It is the honest substitute for a gradient-trained 1D-CNN/GRU/Transformer on a box with no deep-learning framework and 4.5k labelled sequences.

**Judge 1** feasible 3 · rigour 3 · expected value 2 · keep False — fix: Trap: RidgeClassifierCV picks alpha by an internal leave-one-out that is not session-blocked, so 10,000 PPV features on ~3,600 windows fit within-session neighbours; every pre-SETUP window is templated by the engine's own SETUP definition (CHoCH then continuation), so shape 'motifs' are the rule, not information; the shippable form is again a scalar-feature tree, so at best it reports an unreachable ceiling. Fix: if run at all, 5m only, 2,000 kernels, one window length, alpha chosen by nested GroupKFold, and a pre-registered stop (stacked minus tabular AUC < 0.02 in every fold = report and stop); it never gets a candidate slot and its trials still go to the ledger.

**Judge 2** feasible 3 · rigour 4 · expected value 2 · keep False — fix: As proposed (10,000 kernels, 5,326 x 10,000 float32 doubled to float64 by RidgeCV, ~600 MB peak, 45-75 CPU-min) it does not fit beside lab.py at 1.24 GB with 759 MB free, and the prior that 4.5k labelled 60-bar windows beat hand features in intraday index futures is low. Reduce to a single ceiling check: 1m only, L=60, 2,000 kernels (43 MB matrix, RidgeCV in seconds), one probe, harness splitter; if the CPCV 5th-percentile AUC gain over the tabular HGB is < 0.03 the study ends with that number. The distilled window summaries (sign-agreement count, drawdown from window high) can still be offered to the importance study as scalar features.

### deep-sequence-vision-labels (priority 4)

**technique.** Multimodal Claude as a hindsight annotator: rendered IS session charts labelled sideways / trending per hour-bin by vision, with a reliability check; the labels are ground truth for (a) a direct test that sideways hurts Foundation SETUPs and (b) a causal detector distilled into rules.

**what_it_answers.** Whether the user's visual notion of 'sideways' (the one behind 'CHoCH and CHoCH and no breaks') actually marks losing SETUPs on this tape; whether that notion is already captured by mechanical proxies (H2 counts, range/ATR, the HMM chop state) or adds something; and whether a causal detector can see it early enough to skip the trade.

**method.** Rendering (deep_vision_render.py, matplotlib): for a stratified sample of 240 IS sessions (by year and by Foundation session net tercile so both good and bad sessions are present), a 1400x500 PNG of 5-minute candles with a volume panel, hour gridlines at 10:15/11:15/12:15/13:15/14:15 and a price axis; no date, no engine markers, filenames are opaque ids (the map id -> date is kept in a file the labeller does not read). Labelling: Claude reads each image via the Read tool and returns JSON with one label per hour-bin (SIDEWAYS / TREND_UP / TREND_DOWN / MIXED) and one session label; 40 images are re-labelled in a second pass in a different order for Cohen's kappa; a second variant with engine CHoCH/BOS markers drawn is labelled for a further 60 sessions to test whether the markers change the verdict. Reliability gate: kappa < 0.5 stops the study with that finding. Analysis A (the cheap decisive number): Foundation net and win rate of IS SETUPs by the hindsight label of their hour-bin and of the next hour-bin; permutation p; also the same by mechanical hindsight proxies (hour-bin range/ATR, |net move|/range) and their agreement with the vision labels (if a proxy reproduces the label at >= 90% the vision step is redundant and the proxy is used as the target instead). Analysis B (causal detector): at each SETUP bar predict 'this hour-bin is SIDEWAYS' from as-of features (H2 regime counts, range12/36_atr, session_bar, BOCPD run length and HMM filtered P(chop) from the regime study, fz_read, fz_visit_n) with a depth-3 tree and an L1 logistic; gate = skip when P(sideways) >= tau; tau and the tree chosen on purged session-blocked CV by kept-vs-skipped difference and control percentile. Extension to the whole IS: the detector's target for unlabelled sessions is the best mechanical proxy only if step A showed it agrees with vision at >= 90%; otherwise the detector is trained on the 240 labelled sessions only (about 1,000-1,200 SETUPs on 1m, ~200 on 5m) and that smaller n is stated. OOS: sessions are never rendered or labelled before the final run; the frozen detector is scored once on OOS SETUPs; afterwards 40 OOS sessions are rendered and labelled only to report detector-vs-label agreement, never to change anything.

**leakage_controls.** Vision labels are hindsight by design and are used only as targets/diagnostics, never as features; charts carry no date so the labeller cannot recall known events; OOS sessions are not shown to the labeller until after the OOS scoring; the detector uses as-of features only; the session sample is stratified by session net, which is a label, so the stratum is recorded and analysis A is reported both weighted back to the IS session distribution and raw; CV is session-blocked with purge/embargo; the number of tau/tree variants is counted for the family-wise p.

**compute.** Rendering 300 charts ~3 CPU-minutes; detector training seconds; peak memory under 300 MB. The binding budget is tokens: ~300 images at ~1,000-1,500 tokens each, about 350-450k tokens including re-labels, well inside the session budget but slow in wall-clock (one image per Read call).

**libraries.** matplotlib, PIL, pandas, numpy, sklearn (DecisionTreeClassifier, LogisticRegression), the regime study's per-bar outputs if available.

**output_as_config.** A rule list (<= 8 rules, depth <= 3) from the depth-3 tree, e.g. {"if": [["n_choch_since_bos", ">=", 2], ["range36_atr", "<", 1.2], ["session_bar", ">=", 60]], "then": "skip", "source": "causal detector of vision-labelled sideways, IS 2021-10..2025-12, script deep_vision_detector.py, sha", "statistic": "label kappa, CV precision/recall vs the hindsight label, kept-vs-skipped INR/trade, control pct"}; the label set itself (session id -> labels) is kept in the scratchpad as provenance, not in config.

**novelty.** Vision-language models as annotators replacing crowd labelling (Gilardi et al. PNAS 2023 for text; VLM chart annotation work 2024-2025) combined with weak supervision / distillation of a noisy hindsight label into a causal student model (Ratner et al. 2017 data programming; Hinton et al. 2015 distillation); the reliability protocol (double labelling, kappa) is standard annotation practice and is what makes an LLM label admissible as ground truth.

**Judge 1** feasible 3 · rigour 3 · expected value 2 · keep False — fix: Trap: a hindsight 'sideways this hour' label is nearly the outcome (a Foundation trade needs a move), so analysis A confirms itself; the same model re-labelling 40 images is self-consistency, not inter-rater kappa; the mechanical proxy will match at >= 90% and the causal detector collapses to a range forecaster the table already has (range36_atr, session_bar); 300 Read calls of wall-clock for that. Fix: skip rendering; define the hindsight target mechanically (hour-bin range/ATR and net-move/range) and test the causal detector as features inside studies 2/13; if the user's visual notion must be tested, the user labels 40 sessions by hand and agreement with the proxy is the finding.

**Judge 2** feasible 3 · rigour 3 · expected value 3 · keep True — fix: Pilot before committing 350-450k tokens: render 40 IS sessions, label them, compute kappa on 20 re-labels AND agreement with the mechanical proxies (hour-bin range/ATR, |net move|/range, n_choch_since_bos); if any proxy agrees >= 85% the vision step stops and the proxy becomes the target. Drop 5m (about 200 SETUPs in the labelled sample). The labeller must be a separate agent from the LLM-hypothesis generator and from any study author (label contamination across studies). The stratification by session net is a label: report analysis A reweighted to the IS session distribution as the headline, raw as secondary. The detector is a depth-3 tree on the same as-of features as H5, so its only added value is the denoised target; say that in the deliverable.

### deep-sequence-motif-shapelet (priority 5)

**technique.** SETUP-anchored matrix-profile motif discovery (MASS/FFT z-normalised distances), time-series clustering of pre-SETUP shapes (k-means on z-normalised windows and on random-kernel features), and discriminative shapelet search; plus a causal novelty score (distance of the pre-SETUP window to the nearest earlier window on the tape).

**what_it_answers.** Whether recurring pre-SETUP shapes exist (motifs), whether any motif or cluster has a Foundation expectancy that survives a session-blocked permutation test, whether a single short template (a shapelet) with a distance threshold separates losers, and whether 'this looks like nothing seen before' (novelty) or 'this looks exactly like a familiar pattern' predicts the outcome.

**method.** Windows: the direction-normalised close path over the last m bars ending at setup_i (1m: m in {30, 60}; 5m: m in {12, 24}), z-normalised per window; a second representation is the 6-channel tensor of the rocket study. Motifs (deep_motif.py): full pairwise z-normalised Euclidean distance matrix among IS SETUP windows (4,502^2 x 60, seconds via matmul), nearest-neighbour graph, motif chains and agglomerative clustering with a distance cut chosen by the silhouette on training folds; per-cluster Foundation net, win rate, n, and a permutation p where the null shuffles labels within session blocks; clusters with n < 40 are reported but never proposed. Clustering (deep_cluster.py): k-means (k in 4..12) on the z-normalised paths and separately on the PPV features; cluster expectancy tables on CV (the cluster assignment for a test-fold SETUP uses training-fold centroids only); 'loser clusters' are then explained by a depth-3 tree on scalar as-of features (fidelity reported). Shapelets (deep_shapelet.py, numba): 3,000 random candidate subsequences of length 8-20 drawn from training-fold windows; for each, the minimum sliding distance to every window; information gain on fnd_win and the best threshold; the top 20 by gain re-scored on the held-out fold; a shapelet survives if its gain rank is stable across folds and its skip rule has kept-vs-skipped difference > 0 in every fold. Novelty (deep_novelty.py): for each SETUP the MASS distance profile of its m-bar window against the tape strictly before k - m (prefix only, so causal), taking the 1st-percentile distance and the nearest-neighbour distance as scalar features nn_dist_prefix, p1_dist_prefix; their deciles vs Foundation net; they are handed to the rule search of the LLM/H5 studies as candidate scalar features. All selection on purged session-blocked IS CV; family-wise p across every cluster/shapelet/threshold examined; OOS scored once with the frozen motif library, centroids and shapelets from all IS.

**leakage_controls.** Windows end at the SETUP bar; the motif library, centroids and shapelets are built from training folds only inside CV and from all IS for the OOS run (a test window is never a member of the library it is scored against); the novelty score uses the tape prefix strictly before the window (bars <= k - m), never the future; cluster/shapelet counts examined are logged for the family-wise correction; small clusters are excluded from proposals; embargo/purge as in the common protocol; OOS once.

**compute.** Pairwise distances seconds; k-means ~1 s per fit (benchmarked); shapelet search 3,000 candidates x 5,326 windows x ~100 positions x 15 length in numba ~2-4 min per fold; prefix MASS 5,326 queries x 0.19 s ~17 min for 1m (5m ~1 min). Total ~40-50 CPU-minutes for 1m, ~10 for 5m; peak memory under 400 MB (the 4,502^2 float32 distance matrix is 81 MB).

**libraries.** numpy, scipy.signal.fftconvolve (MASS), numba (shapelet distances), sklearn (KMeans, AgglomerativeClustering, DecisionTreeClassifier), pandas.

**output_as_config.** Two forms. Compact and preferred: scalar-feature rules (from the cluster-explaining tree or from the novelty deciles), e.g. {"if": [["nn_dist_prefix", ">", 4.2]], "then": "skip"} where nn_dist_prefix needs a small as-of routine (window length m, prefix MASS) added to the fz code under a config key {"novelty": {"window": 30, "skip_if_nn_dist_gt": 4.2}}. Alternative requiring a user decision: a shapelet template of <= 20 numbers plus a threshold {"shape_skip": {"template": [...], "max_dist": 1.8, "window": 30}}; a rule with a 20-number template is arguably compact but it is a new key type, so it is offered, not assumed.

**novelty.** Matrix profile and MASS (Yeh et al. ICDM 2016; Mueen 2015; STOMP/SCRIMP 2017-2018), motif and discord discovery as the current standard for unsupervised pattern mining in long series; shapelets (Ye & Keogh 2009), learned/random shapelets (Grabocka et al. 2014; Karlsson et al. 2016 random shapelet forests); k-Shape and time-series clustering benchmarks (Paparrizos & Gravano 2015; Javed et al. 2020). The causal prefix-novelty score is a matrix-profile discord applied online.

**Judge 1** feasible 4 · rigour 3 · expected value 1 · keep False — fix: Trap: 3,000 random shapelets x thresholds x ~30 clusters is a family 184 winners cannot pay for, every pre-SETUP path shares the engine's break-then-continue template, and a 20-number template as config is a new key type no practitioner would ship from this sample. Fix: compute only nn_dist_prefix and p1_dist_prefix (causal prefix MASS, ~17 minutes on 1m) as two scalar candidates for study 3; drop motif, cluster and shapelet search entirely.

**Judge 2** feasible 4 · rigour 4 · expected value 2 · keep False — fix: Z-normalised shape clusters and random shapelets on 15%-positive labels add ~3,000+ trials to the ledger with a low prior, and both deployable forms (20-number template, prefix-MASS routine) are new key types needing a user decision. Keep only the causal novelty scores nn_dist_prefix and p1_dist_prefix (prefix MASS, ~17 min on 1m, ~1 min on 5m, numba/fftconvolve present) as two candidate scalar features handed to the importance study; drop the motif and shapelet searches.

### deep-sequence-pretrain-probe (priority 6)

**technique.** Self-supervised representation learning on all IS bars, framework-free: a ladder of PCA (the linear autoencoder), an sklearn MLP bottleneck autoencoder, and a forecast-pretext encoder (predict the next 30-bar normalised path and realised range from the past 60), each followed by a linear probe on SETUP embeddings; the embedding is then clustered and the clusters distilled to rules.

**what_it_answers.** Whether pretraining on the whole tape (445k 1m windows, most of them not SETUPs) yields a representation of the pre-SETUP window in which Foundation winners and losers are linearly separable better than in the raw features or the PCA baseline, i.e. whether the 'foundation-model' idea has any purchase at this scale without a deep-learning framework; and, if so, whether the useful directions can be named.

**method.** Pretraining windows: every IS bar k (subsampled to ~120k windows stratified by session, float32) with the same 6-channel 60-bar window as the rocket study (direction-normalised with sg = +1, since pretraining has no direction; the SETUP-time embedding is computed for the window as seen and, separately, for its sign-flipped version for down SETUPs). Ladder: (1) PCA to 16 components on the flattened window (linear autoencoder baseline); (2) MLPRegressor(96, 16, 96) autoencoder, identity target, early stopping on a held-out session block, embedding = the 16-unit layer read from coefs_; (3) forecast-pretext encoder: MLPRegressor(96, 16) whose targets are the z-normalised next-30-bar close path and log realised range (both computed from bars after k, used only as pretraining targets on IS bars of the training folds), embedding = the 16-unit layer. Linear probe: L2 logistic regression / ridge on the embedding of each SETUP window for fnd_win and winsorised net; comparators in the same folds: raw z-scored window logistic, tabular H5 xgboost, and stacked (tabular + embedding). Evaluation: AUC, kept-vs-skipped difference at 30/50/70% skip, pooled control percentile, on purged session-blocked IS CV where the pretraining set excludes the test block's sessions and the embargo. Understanding step: k-means (k=8) on the SETUP embeddings, cluster expectancy tables, and a depth-3 tree on scalar as-of features to explain each loser cluster (fidelity reported); also the correlation of each embedding dimension with named as-of features so that the useful directions are described in words. OOS: encoder refit on all IS, frozen, one run.

**leakage_controls.** Pretraining windows end at their own bar; the forecast-pretext targets use the future only inside pretraining on training-fold IS bars, never at inference and never from test-fold or OOS sessions (pretraining per fold excludes the test block plus embargo); PCA/AE/encoder and scaling fit on training folds; the SETUP's own label never enters pretraining; purged/embargoed session-blocked CV; the number of ladder rungs x probes is counted for the family-wise p; OOS once. The known trap that a forecast-pretext embedding of window k encodes information about bars after k is closed by construction: at inference the encoder only sees bars <= k.

**compute.** Benchmarked MLPRegressor 30k x 60 for 20 epochs in 9 s; 120k windows x 360 inputs x ~150 epochs is ~25-40 min per model, two models per fold is too much for 5 folds, so pretraining is done on 3 folds only (3 contiguous blocks) plus the all-IS refit: ~3-4 CPU-hours for 1m in total, ~30 min for 5m (12-bar windows); PCA and probes seconds. Peak memory ~750 MB (120k x 360 float64 copy inside sklearn = 345 MB plus gradients); must run alone while the lab rebuild holds 1.5 GB, or with 80k windows.

**libraries.** numpy, sklearn (PCA, MLPRegressor, LogisticRegression, Ridge, KMeans, DecisionTreeClassifier), pandas, pyarrow for the parquet reads; no torch (absent, not installable) so no contrastive InfoNCE objective; this is stated as the reason the ladder stops at a forecast pretext.

**output_as_config.** Only the distilled rule list from the cluster-explaining tree is proposed for ST13/ST14 (<= 8 rules of depth <= 3, provenance 'distilled from pretrain probe, script deep_pretrain.py, sha'); the encoder weights are never config. If the embedding beats PCA and tabular by less than 300 INR/trade on CV, the study's deliverable is the negative result and a recommendation not to pursue sequence pretraining until torch is available and the tape is longer.

**novelty.** Self-supervised time-series representation learning with a linear-probe evaluation: TS2Vec (Yue et al. AAAI 2022), TS-TCC (Eldele et al. 2021), CoST (2022), masked/forecast pretext pretraining in PatchTST (Nie et al. ICLR 2023) and the time-series foundation models MOMENT and Chronos (2024); the linear autoencoder = PCA equivalence (Baldi & Hornik 1989) and deep autoencoders (Hinton & Salakhutdinov 2006) give the honest baseline the ladder must beat. The forecast pretext is the framework-free stand-in for contrastive pretraining.

**Judge 1** feasible 2 · rigour 3 · expected value 1 · keep False — fix: Trap: a 16-unit sklearn MLP autoencoder is nonlinear PCA, not a foundation model; 3-4 CPU-hours at 750 MB on a box already carrying another session's build, to confirm the negative the study itself predicts, with 'embedding dimension correlates with a named feature' read as discovery. Fix: do not run; a PCA-16 linear probe (seconds) as a footnote in study 9 if that runs; revisit only with torch and a longer tape.

**Judge 2** feasible 2 · rigour 4 · expected value 1 · keep False — fix: 3-4 CPU-hours at ~750 MB peak cannot run beside lab.py (759 MB free) and would block the box; sklearn MLPRegressor with 360 inputs on 120k rows is the wrong tool and the study itself predicts a negative result. Run only rung 1 (PCA-16 on the flattened window, seconds) as the linear baseline inside the reduced rocket check; record 'sequence pretraining deferred until torch is available' in the provenance notes.

### quant-ml-canon-purged-cpcv-pbo (priority 1)

**technique.** Validation harness: purged k-fold with embargo keyed on label end time, Combinatorial Purged Cross-Validation (CPCV) backtest paths, CSCV probability of backtest overfitting (PBO), deflated Sharpe ratio, minimum backtest length, stationary block bootstrap of sessions, and a shared trials ledger for every candidate gate from H1-H5 and these studies.

**what_it_answers.** Are the in-sample improvements of any candidate gate (rooms ST7/ST8, H2 regime, H3 volume, H4 levels, H5 rule lists, the studies below) larger than what picking the best of N trials produces by chance on 823 (5m) or a few thousand (1m) SETUPs? Which candidates have earned the single out-of-sample look? How many candidates can this sample afford before selection bias dominates?

### quant-ml-canon-feature-importance (priority 3)

**technique.** Clustered feature importance (MDA and MDI on correlation clusters, single-feature importance per cluster) under purged cross-validation, the orthogonal-feature sanity check, fractional differentiation (fixed-width FFD with a hand-coded ADF choice of d*) as a candidate feature family, and TreeSHAP interaction values for discovering the 2-3-feature interactions that justify depth-2/3 rules.

**what_it_answers.** Which of the 143 as-of columns (97 numeric, 114 pairs with |rho| > 0.9, so raw importances would be split by substitution) carry information about the intraday Foundation outcome; which at most 8 feature clusters the ST13/ST14 keys should be built from and what their config-expressible representative is; whether the canon's fractionally differentiated price features add anything beyond the ATR-normalised distances already in the table; which feature pairs interact strongly enough to justify a depth-2/3 rule, so that H5's rule search can be restricted (fewer trials, lower PBO).

**method.** (1) Feature set: as-of numeric plus encoded categoricals as in the meta-label study; NaN kept for HGB, a missing-indicator plus median for bagging; columns with > 40% NaN dropped and listed. (2) FFD family: on log close per timeframe, fixed-width fractional differentiation for d in {0.1, 0.2, ..., 1.0} with weight cut 1e-4 (window length recorded); ADF regression with constant and 2 lags hand-coded in numpy (MacKinnon 5% critical value -2.86 embedded and cited); d* = smallest d whose ADF statistic on IS bars is below the critical value; features at the SETUP bar: ffd_close_d*, its z-score against a 60-bar rolling std, the same on log volume; plus the CUSUM-filter counts from the regime study when available. (3) Clustering: Spearman correlation on IS, distance sqrt(0.5 (1 - rho)), scipy average linkage, cut chosen by silhouette in the 20-40 cluster range; every cluster gets a representative chosen by config-expressibility (integer counts and card fields before ATR ratios before raw points). (4) Model and protocol: BaggingClassifier of depth-limited trees on the L1 label with |net| weights under the harness splitter (12 purged, embargoed blocks). Clustered MDA: permute all features of a cluster jointly on the test fold, 5 permutations x 12 folds, record the drop in OOF weighted log-loss and, separately, the drop in OOF kept-vs-skipped expectancy at the fold's tau (the metric that matters); report mean and std across folds; a cluster is kept only when its mean MDA exceeds one std. MDI from the same forest summed per cluster; SFI: each cluster alone under the same CV. (5) Orthogonal check: PCA on standardised IS features, weighted Kendall tau between MDI rank and eigenvalue rank; a low tau is reported as a warning that the importance may be noise-fitting. ### quant-ml-canon-conformal-skip (priority 4)

**technique.** Class-conditional (Mondrian) cross-conformal prediction on the meta-model's nonconformity score, with the skip decision defined as 'win is not in the prediction set', a Mondrian taxonomy by class x time bin for the user's 'different for different time', and Adaptive Conformal Inference for drift in the live/walk-forward version.

**what_it_answers.** The user's acceptance criterion made formal: can SETUPs be skipped with a finite-sample guarantee that at most alpha of the true winners are skipped (marginally over SETUPs, under exchangeability), what skip rate, loser recall and kept-vs-skipped expectancy each alpha buys on IS out-of-fold, whether the winner coverage holds on OOS, what the hour-of-day guarantee costs in sample size, and how far a coverage-chosen threshold sits from the expectancy-chosen threshold of the meta-label study.

**method.** Score s(x) = 1 - p_win_hat(x) taken from the meta-label study's IS out-of-fold probabilities (the scorecard variant, since the forest cannot live in the lab; the forest's scores are reported as reference). Cross-conformal / CV+ flavour: no separate calibration split is sacrificed (181 winners on 5m IS cannot afford one); within the harness's 12 purged folds, the conformal quantile is computed on the OOF scores of the winners in 11 folds and applied to the 12th, so the IS evaluation is itself out-of-fold. Mondrian by class: q_alpha = the ceil((n_win + 1)(1 - alpha))-th smallest OOF score among true winners; the prediction set contains 'win' iff s(x) <= q_alpha; gate = skip iff 'win' is not in the set, i.e. p_win_hat(x) < 1 - q_alpha. Guarantee: P(win in set | true winner) >= 1 - alpha, so at most alpha of winners are skipped in expectation; nothing is guaranteed for losers, their recall is measured. Alpha grid {0.05, 0.10, 0.15, 0.20, 0.30}; the smallest feasible alpha is 1/(n_win + 1) (0.0055 on 5m IS). Time taxonomy: class x time bin with three bins fixed in advance (09:15-10:29, 10:30-12:59, 13:00-15:19; the IS hour profile shows morning losses and afternoon gains); feasibility stated from winner counts per bin (5m IS: 16-39 winners per hour, so per-bin guarantees only for alpha >= 0.1; on 1m roughly 4-5x more). Robustness: |net|-weighted coverage (share of winners' gross net kept) reported next to count coverage, because the guarantee is per SETUP, not per rupee. Evaluation on IS-OOF per alpha: skip rate, winner coverage, loser recall, kept-vs-skipped expectancy and permutation p, control percentile (fz_report.random_control), top-decile-winner coverage; alpha chosen on IS by the pre-registered rule 'largest loser recall subject to winner coverage >= 0.9 and kept-vs-skipped difference > 0'. Exchangeability check: coverage per IS year and against the regime study's break tests. ACI variant (Gibbs and Candes): alpha_t+1 = alpha_t + gamma (alpha - err_t), gamma in {0.005, 0.01}, run sequentially over IS in time order, then continued into OOS from IS's final state without re-tuning, to see whether an online alpha keeps coverage under drift. OOS once: the frozen (alpha, taxonomy, thresholds) and the frozen ACI state, same table.

**leakage_controls.** Scores are out-of-fold from the purged, embargoed splitter; the conformal quantile uses only winners from calibration folds; alpha, the time bins and the selection rule are fixed before OOS; ACI's state carries forward without any OOS tuning; the study reads no OOS row until the single frozen run; the comparison with the expectancy-chosen tau is on IS-OOF only.

**compute.** Quantiles over <= 5k scores x 5 alphas x 12 folds and a sequential ACI loop over <= 5k rows: under 1 CPU-minute, under 100 MB; it depends only on the meta-label study's OOF file.

**libraries.** numpy, pandas; mapie is not installed and not needed (split/cross-conformal quantile and ACI are about 40 lines); fz_report.random_control / permutation_p read-only.

**output_as_config.** A "conformal" block in ST13/ST14: {"alpha": 0.10, "taxonomy": "class" or "class_x_timebin", "time_bins": [["09:15","10:29"],["10:30","12:59"],["13:00","15:19"]], "skip_if_score_below": s (per bin, the deployable scorecard's score at 1 - q_alpha), "calibration": {"n_winners": n, "is_window": "2021-10-01..2025-12-31", "oof_scores_sha": ...}, "aci": {"enabled": false or true, "gamma": 0.01, "alpha_state": a}}. The lab compares the scorecard score with a number; nothing else is code. Because the threshold is a single number per bin, it is the most compact form any gate in this study set can take.

### decision-making-2-exit-policy-fqi-exomdp (priority 2)

**technique.** Offline sequential RL for the in-trade exit on an exogenous tape: Fitted Q-Iteration with tree ensembles and a pessimistic (ensemble lower-bound) value, plus hindsight-imitation from the exactly computable optimal exit, evaluated by exact replay (with doubly-robust OPE only where fills are uncertain) and distilled to the lab's position keys

**what_it_answers.** How to exit: per bar, hold / exit all / exit one of three lots / move the stop to break-even, as a function of the trade's own path (R so far, MFE/MAE so far, bars held, time to square-off), the card read now, room-edge distances, the regime counters and volume; whether such a policy beats the Foundation next-CHoCH exit and the ST9/ST10 R-ladder on the same entries, and how far it is from the hindsight-optimal exit.

**method.** Trajectories: for every Foundation SETUP (IS: 1m 4,502, 5m 832) walk the bars from entry+1 to the entry session's last bar at or before 15:25, with the Foundation stop (prev_swing on 1m, choch_candle on 5m, touch rule, engine fill convention) as a hard floor that ends the episode; this extends the logged trajectory beyond the Foundation exit so that every state has both actions' consequences on the tape (Exo-MDP: the tape does not react to a 65-lot order). State s_t (all as-of bar t): bars_held, unrealised pts / SL distance and / atr14, mfe_R, mae_R, drawdown from MFE in R, session_bar and bars to square-off, atr14, vol_ratio20 at t, card read at t (one-hot from card.csv), room edge ahead/behind in ATR from zones.csv (alive at t), n_choch_since_bos at t and whether a CHoCH against the position has printed since entry (events.csv, i <= t), lots remaining. Whole-trade quantities (fnd_mfe, fnd_bars_held, exit reason) never enter a state; only so-far versions. The oracle label is a training target for learner B only and never a feature; learner B's threshold and A's pessimism weight are chosen on IS CV. ### decision-making-3-robustness-resampling-multiplicity-adversarial (priority 3)

### decision-making-4-selective-gate-conformal-risk-control (priority 4)

**technique.** Selective classification with a reject option and conformal risk control: a distribution-free finite-sample certificate on the loss of the kept trades, the winner-retained vs loser-avoided Pareto front, and cost-assumption sensitivity, to fix the gate's operating point

**what_it_answers.** Where to set the gate's threshold so that it "avoids only loss trades and not profitable ones" in a quantified, certified way: for a chosen coverage (share of SETUPs kept) what mean loss / loser share among kept trades can be guaranteed, how much winner-net is given up, and whether the chosen point survives slippage of 3 / 5 / 8 pts per side.

**method.** Score: the out-of-fold IS score from study 1 (E[net|x] or weighted P(win)). (i) Selective risk-coverage curves on IS CV: coverage c in [0.1, 1.0], selective risk = mean of clipped loss L = min(max(-net, 0), 6,000 INR) among kept, and separately the loser share; the curve is the decision surface the user chooses on. (ii) Conformal Risk Control (Angelopoulos, Bates, Candes, Jordan & Lei 2022): split IS by session blocks into train (for the score) and a calibration block (>= 200 sessions); for a target risk alpha, pick the largest coverage lambda whose calibration risk (with the +B/(n+1) correction, B = the clip) is <= alpha; the guarantee E[risk of kept] <= alpha holds under exchangeability of calibration units, which here are sessions (aggregate to session-level losses so weak dependence within a day does not break the units); report alpha in {800, 1,000, 1,200 INR} (the cost level: 1m mean cost 1,048-1,116 INR). (iii) Cost-aware operating point: utility U_w = sum kept net - w * sum over skipped of max(net, 0) for w in {0, 0.5, 1}; the Pareto front (winner-net retained %, loser-net avoided %) is presented and the user picks w (Rule 0(c): the operating point is a user decision, not a fit). (iv) Sensitivity: recompute every net at slippage 3 / 5 / 8 pts per side and with the brokerage cap at 20 vs 0.03% uncapped; the chosen point must keep a positive kept-minus-skipped difference at 8 pts. OOS once: realised selective risk vs the certified alpha (does the certificate hold?), kept share vs the planned coverage, kept/skipped expectancy, control percentile.

**leakage_controls.** Scores come from out-of-fold IS predictions only (never in-sample fits); the calibration block is disjoint in time from the score's training folds and sits after them with a 2-session embargo; the clip B and the risk definition are fixed above; w is chosen by the user on IS curves before OOS is opened; OOS is evaluated once at the frozen (lambda, w).

**compute.** Seconds to a few minutes: numpy sweeps over <= 5k rows, 2,000 control draws, four slippage variants.

**libraries.** numpy, pandas, scipy, scikit-learn 1.7.2 (calibration utilities), matplotlib for the risk-coverage plot in the report

**output_as_config.** strategy_13/14.json "gate" additions: {"operating_point": {"coverage": lambda, "min_expected_net_inr": tau_at_lambda, "certified_risk_inr": alpha, "clip_inr": 6000, "calibration_sessions": n, "winner_weight_w": w}, "cost_assumptions": {"slippage_pts_per_side": 5, "survives_slippage_pts": 8}} with provenance. The certificate numbers are documentation of the guarantee, the two thresholds are the only trading numbers.

**novelty.** Chow 1970 (reject option) and Geifman & El-Yaniv 2017 (selective classification, risk-coverage) frame skip as abstention with a known zero cost; Angelopoulos et al. 2022 (Conformal Risk Control) and Bates et al. 2021 (RCPS) give the finite-sample guarantee that a plain threshold on a GBM score does not; the cost-aware utility follows the risk-sensitive decision literature (Rockafellar & Uryasev 2000 CVaR as the alternative risk if the user prefers tail control to mean loss). This is the study that turns the user's sentence about bands into a number with a guarantee attached.

### decision-making-5-kelly-bounded-sizing (priority 5)

**technique.** Position sizing as a Kelly-bounded policy: fractional / risk-constrained Kelly on the calibrated conditional edge, with estimation shrinkage from session-block bootstrap, mapped to a monotone lots-by-edge step function

**what_it_answers.** Whether, once a positive-conditional-edge region exists, sizing 1-3 lots by predicted edge improves log-growth and drawdown over flat sizing at the same average lots, and what the bounded, monotone lots schedule is; or, honestly, that the edge is not positive enough anywhere and the answer is 0 lots (the raw book has win rate 15% and mean -922 INR, i.e. negative Kelly).

**method.** Inputs: out-of-fold IS predictions from study 1 (E[net|x] plus 0.1/0.5/0.9 quantiles from HistGradientBoostingRegressor with loss='quantile'), realised net per SETUP, capital C = futures_margin 120,000 x 3 lots + 60,000 buffer (from the strategy files), per-lot charges from lab.trade_charges (brokerage cap makes cost slightly sublinear in lots). Bucket SETUPs by out-of-fold edge decile; per bucket estimate the return-on-capital distribution by stationary session-block bootstrap (2,000 draws) and compute the Kelly fraction f* = argmax E[log(1 + f R)] (scipy scalar optimisation; R = net per lot / C), the risk-constrained variant with a drawdown constraint P(drawdown > 20%) <= 0.1 (Busseti-Ryu-Boyd bound, 1-D per bucket), and the shrunk fraction f_s = 0.5 x the bootstrap 25th percentile of f*. Lots(bucket) = clip(floor(f_s x C / margin_per_lot), 0, 3), then enforced monotone non-decreasing in the edge bucket with <= 3 steps (isotonic step fit). Evaluate by replay with the lab's lock (one open position per contract) and 15:25 square-off on IS CV: terminal log-growth, max drawdown, Sharpe-like t on daily net, vs flat 1-lot, flat 3-lot and a lots-matched flat book (same total lots traded). OOS once with the frozen schedule; also report the schedule's value when study 1's gate is off (sizing on the raw book) to show it cannot rescue a negative edge.

**leakage_controls.** Edge estimates are out-of-fold IS predictions only; the schedule (buckets, steps) is frozen before OOS; the bootstrap is by session blocks so within-day dependence is kept; lots are capped at 3 and the schedule is monotone with <= 3 steps so the sizing rule cannot encode more than a coarse ordering of the edge; the sizing evaluation uses the same 15:25 cut and lock as the lab.

**compute.** < 2 CPU-min, < 200 MB (numpy/scipy on <= 5k rows x 2,000 draws).

**libraries.** numpy, pandas, scipy.optimize, scikit-learn 1.7.2 (HistGradientBoostingRegressor quantile loss, IsotonicRegression)

**output_as_config.** strategy_13/14.json "position": {"lots": 3, "size_by_edge": [{"edge_min_inr": 0, "lots": 1}, {"edge_min_inr": 800, "lots": 2}, {"edge_min_inr": 2000, "lots": 3}], "kelly": {"fraction": 0.5, "capital_inr": 420000, "drawdown_cap": 0.2}} with provenance; "edge" is the same rule-list score study 1 ships, so no new feature is needed in the lab. **novelty.** Kelly 1956 and Thorp 2006 (growth-optimal sizing); MacLean, Thorp & Ziemba 2010 (fractional Kelly and its risk-growth trade-off); Busseti, Ryu & Boyd 2016 (risk-constrained Kelly gambling with a drawdown constraint as a convex problem); Baker & McHale 2013 (Kelly under parameter uncertainty, shrinkage). ### decision-making-6-session-stop-rule-finite-horizon (priority 6)

**method.** State at each SETUP: (today_n_stops_asof, today_net_asof bucket, today_n_setups_before, hour_bin) plus the study-1 score.
